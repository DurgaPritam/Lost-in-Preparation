## Preparation model (M4)

Sources: `data/interim/m4/logp_validation.json`, `data/interim/m4/artemisinin_check.json`, `data/interim/m4/m4_summary.json`

**Solvent descriptor validation** against experimental logP (SangsterLogP (Zenodo 19387552, CC BY 4.0), neutral subset; 150 compounds, xTB succeeded for 150):

| Method | MAE | RMSE | Spearman ρ |
|---|---|---|---|
| RDKit Crippen logP | 0.657 (0.575–0.742) | 0.837 | 0.832 (0.762–0.885) |
| GFN2-xTB/ALPB (xtb 6.7.1), woctanol − water | 1.505 (1.317–1.710) | 1.924 | 0.688 (0.596–0.762) |

**Training-overlap check.** RDKit's Crippen logP follows Wildman & Crippen, J. Chem. Inf. Comput. Sci. 39, 868-873 (1999); its training molecules are not distributed with RDKit, so exact overlap is **not determinable**. Proxy: compounds whose every cited measurement source was first published in 2000 or later (n = 67) vs earlier (n = 43; 40 with an unresolved year):

| Subset | RDKit MAE | xTB MAE |
|---|---|---|
| sources from 2000 on | 0.824 (0.699–0.950) | 1.482 (1.249–1.727) |
| sources before 2000 | 0.530 (0.398–0.672) | 1.928 (1.496–2.436) |

RDKit's error is larger on post-1999 data, which is consistent with (but does not prove) a training-overlap advantage on older data; RDKit still outperforms xTB on the post-1999 subset. xTB logP = log K(wet octanol / water) from ALPB `woctanol` and `water`.

Default descriptor: **rdkit** (lower MAE). Caveat: Crippen's parameters were fitted to public logP data that may overlap this set; xTB is unfitted. 95% CIs are paired bootstrap (B = 2000).

**Artemisinin known case: BORDERLINE PASS.** Descriptor rdkit (logP 2.40). Endoperoxide heat flag: True.
