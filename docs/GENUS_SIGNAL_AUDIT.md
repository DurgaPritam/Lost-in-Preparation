# Classical-genus signal audit

Generated by `python -m lip m2 resolve` (`pipeline/lip/m2/genus_audit.py`); do not edit by hand. Findings are AI-reviewed (`ai_drafted_unverified`) and await the project lead's check.

## Why

Name resolution v2 boosts candidates in a modern genus whose name matches a word of the historical name (e.g. 'MANDRAGORA' → *Mandragora*). That goes wrong when:
- the word has a different classical meaning (Pliny's *laser* is the juice of silphium, not the modern genus *Laser*);
- an English word spell-matches a genus (Culpeper's 'Cotton Thistle' → *Cotinus*);
- the 'name' is a chapter title.

## Screen

Every top-1 resolution whose evidence includes a classical-genus match was screened: **417** resolutions (404 Pliny, 13 Culpeper). In 127 of them the word equals the genus name; in the rest it is a variant or spelling match. Each word was looked up in Lewis & Short (PerseusDL TEI, CC BY-SA 4.0): does not mention genus 198, no entry 118, mentions genus 101. A dictionary entry that does not name the genus is only a prompt for review, since many entries give no Linnaean name.
