Honesty treatments · used identically on every page

Eight ways the interface stays honest

Every status is carried by shape, line style and a printed word; colour is the redundant fourth channel. Tooltips explain, never hold the only copy. All numbers below are mock placeholders.

1 · EvidenceStatusBadge

Evidence status

Provisional AI-generated reference. Not yet checked by a person. Read it as a lead, not a finding.
Filled · half · dashed ring. Solid · dashed · dotted border. Focusable, so the tooltip opens from the keyboard.
2 · SourceConfidenceMarker

Source confidence, per source

Never averaged into one score. Two pips + solid = higher; one pip + dashed = lower. Both in the historical family; the meter carries the level.

The band is the value; the point is a thin tick. A wide band is drawn at full weight, because it is a finding too. Read aloud: “between 0.11 and 0.88, range width 0.77”.
5 · ExtractionDetectionExplainer

Extraction is not detection

These are predicted extraction likelihoods, not lab detection. Why it matters
Step 1 · outside the modelPlant contentHow much of the compound the material held. Varies with species, part, season, storage.
Step 2 · modelledExtractionWhether vehicle, heat, time and filtering would carry the compound into the liquid. This is what we estimate.
Step 3 · outside the modelLab detectionWhether an assay would find it. Depends on content, extraction and the method’s limit of detection.
